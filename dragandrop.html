CONTEXT

WHAT IS DRAG AND DROP

Drag and drop is a core Windows interaction that lets people move files between folders, share content through apps, or open it in another tool. It’s one of the most used gestures in Windows.

Business Objective

Redesign drag and drop in Windows into a fluid, context-aware experience that understands user intent and makes moving, sharing, and acting on files faster across people, apps, and devices.

My Role

Led the concept from hackathon to product: shaped the vision, designed the interactions and visuals, prototyped, aligned stakeholders, and worked with engineering through delivery.

USAGE OF DRAG AND DROP

PROBLEMS

USER CHALLENGES

Friction before drag

Users find the current way of dragging and dropping lengthy because of the pre-work needed before they can start dragging.

Poor drop affordance

When dragging a file, users are shown options that aren’t droppable or have smaller hit areas.

Outdated workflows

User needs and workflows have evolved significantly, but the underlying interactions haven’t kept pace.

HYPOTHESIS

We believe that showing relevant drop targets and reducing complexity will improve drag-and-drop success and increase overall drag-and-drop usage.

DESIGN DIRECTION

A SIMPLER PATH FROM DRAG TO DROP

We focused first on sharing and moving files, which accounted for the majority of drag-and-drop activity. Three principles guided our explorations and helped us evaluate each direction.

EXPLORATIONS

THE TASKBAR AS DROP TRAY

The Taskbar as Drop Tray
Transforms the taskbar into a tray of suggested destinations when a user drags a file.
What it enables
Builds on the familiar behavior of dragging files to the taskbar, bringing apps, devices, and people into one place without adding a separate drop area.
What it trades off
Replacing taskbar icons disrupts familiar positions and window access. Suggested destinations also add noise for users who already know where to drop.
Design Takeaway
Expand destinations without displacing existing targets. The tray should extend the taskbar without disrupting familiar drag-and-drop workflows.

DROP MODE

Drop mode
Dragging gathers open windows into one overview, with a tray for extra destinations: people, devices, apps, and folders.
What it enables
Makes open windows easier to see and target without small taskbar icons or overlapping windows. Extra destinations support sharing and moving files beyond what’s open.
What it trades off
Reorganizing the desktop on every drag can interrupt simple drops, force users to relocate known destinations, and add complexity.
Design Takeaway
Larger targets ease dropping, but the mode should preserve context and not interrupt simple workflows. It also offers a scalable base for smart file actions and future capabilities.

DROP TRAY ENTRY POINT ON THE TASKBAR

Drop Tray Entry Point on the Taskbar
Adds a dedicated entry point during a drag. Hovering over it opens a tray of destinations for sharing or moving files, while keeping existing taskbar apps available.
What it enables
Preserves familiar taskbar targets while providing access to people, apps, devices, and folders. Users can share files or move them to a folder without opening the destination first.
What it trades off
An entry point that appears only during a drag may be easy to miss. Accessing the tray requires an additional hover step, while existing taskbar icons remain small and difficult to target.
Design Takeaway
Extend drag-and-drop options while preserving familiar targets. A dedicated entry point supports both sharing and moving, though its discoverability and the additional hover step introduce friction.

DROP TO HOLD

Drop to hold
Introduces a temporary holding space within the drop tray, allowing users to collect files from different locations and act on them later.
What it enables
Lets users build a collection while continuing their work, without creating temporary folders or committing to an immediate action.
What it trades off
Expands drag and drop into a workflow that supports collection, pause, and continuation. Separating collection from action gives users control over when they are ready to proceed.

SOLUTION

THE DROP TRAY

Drop Tray is a Windows surface that appears when you start dragging a file. It brings relevant destinations for sharing and moving files into one place, making it easier to find where to drop.
