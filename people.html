CONTEXT

PEOPLE IN WINDOWS

People in Windows brings the people you talk to into Windows itself. Contacts from apps like WhatsApp and Phone Link come into the OS. In Universal Composer, you type a name, find the right person, and call or message them without opening another app.

My Role

I owned the design for People in Universal Composer. I defined the end-to-end people experience in UC, from interaction to visual design. I worked closely with my PM and engineering partners and aligned the work with the Universal Composer team.

Why it matters

A disconnected ecosystem is the second-biggest reason people leave Windows, and communication is at the center of it. People in Windows closes that gap by bringing the apps people already use into the OS, so reaching someone starts on your PC, not your phone.

THE PROBLEM

People are central to how Windows is used and how its users communicate. Their habits, workflows and needs shape how they interact with the system, from the apps they rely on to how they collaborate.

OPPORTUNITY FOR WINDOWS

Windows can make connecting and collaborating faster and more natural, by turning each person into a context-rich entity, built from their communication and activity, and bringing it natively into the system.

PEOPLE IN FILE EXPLORER

WPC in File Explorer builds on users’ natural association between files and the people they collaborate with.

Users can instantly connect (call, message, or email) based on available contact details, without leaving File Explorer.

PEOPLE IN FILE WSB

People in WSB allows users to search for their contacts and initiate communication actions, as well as view communication details, from the hub of Windows.

It will also surface activity, common groups, shared media and files, and related profiles.

PEOPLE ON THE WIDGET BOARD

Quickly connect with your top contacts, directly from the desktop.
